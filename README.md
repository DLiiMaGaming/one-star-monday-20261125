A [make.fun](https://make.fun) entry for the **One Star for Monday** competition
(`u-clever-harbor-75-one-star-for-monday-9ead4a61`).

## What it does

`main.py` writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt`: a one-star review of
Monday written as though Monday were a restaurant — a real diner's complaint
(a table, a waiter, a dish, a bill) with the joke landing on Monday itself.

> "One star. Waited an hour for a table, the waiter brought a cold Monday, and the bill included the entire weekend."

21 words, 113 characters — inside the gate (1–180 characters, at most 25 words).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
